Save the three photographs of the building in THIS folder, under exactly these names:

    facade-front.jpg    the straight-on shot of the frontage with the signboard
    facade-aerial.jpg   the elevated shot showing the building and the forecourt
    facade-side.jpg     the angled early-morning shot

The first one is the main hero image; the other two become the thumbnails under it.

Landscape, about 2000px wide, under 500 KB each. They are shown full frame and are
never cropped, so whatever ratio you save is what appears.

To change the names, the captions or the order, edit `heroImages` in
src/data/clinic.ts. Set it to an empty array [] and the hero falls back to a
designed navy glass panel until the photos are ready.
