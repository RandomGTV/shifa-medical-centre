Drop the clinic's photos into this folder.

Then open src/data/gallery.ts and add the path to the matching item, e.g.

  {
    id: "op-wing",
    title: "Outpatient wing",
    category: "Facility",
    caption: "...",
    image: "/gallery/op-wing.jpg",   <-- add this line
  }

Any item without an "image" keeps rendering its designed placeholder tile, so you can
swap photos in one at a time without the layout ever breaking.

Suggested sizes: landscape 1600x1200, portrait 1200x1600, under 400 KB each (JPG or WebP).
